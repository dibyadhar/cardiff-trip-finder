    Sweet bake

    Welsh cakes

    Small griddle-cooked cakes with currants and warm spice, finished with a dusting of sugar.

    Try them: Fresh and slightly warm. Usually vegetarian; commonly contains gluten, dairy and egg.

    Comfort food

    Cawl

    A warming Welsh broth or stew, traditionally made with lamb or beef, leeks and root vegetables.

    Best for: A cool or rainy day. Recipes vary, so ask about meat and allergens.

    Sea vegetable

    Laverbread

    Slow-cooked laver seaweed with a deep savoury flavour, often served as part of a Welsh breakfast.

    Try it: In patties or alongside eggs; breakfast pairings may include meat or shellfish.

    A crumbly Welsh cheese with a fresh, lightly tangy character and a creamy layer near the rind.

    Look for it: At markets and specialist cheese counters; ask about the producer and rennet.

    Recommended four-day journey

    A practical route from the Cardiff stay, grouped by geography to reduce backtracking. Driving times are approximate; check live weather, tides, road conditions, opening times and bookings before departure.

    Open original PDF
    DAY 1

    Central Bannau Brycheiniog

    Pen y Fan · Craig Cerrig-gleisiad · Brecon Canal Basin

    1. 07:15Leave CF10 3EA for Pont ar Daf. Allow approximately 1 hr 05-20.
    2. 08:30-12:15Pen y Fan summit walk. Allow 3-4 hours including weather pauses and photos.
    3. 12:15-13:00Drive via the A470 to Craig Cerrig-gleisiad; take a picnic or quick cafe stop.
    4. 13:00-14:00Roadside viewpoint or short reserve walk at Craig Cerrig-gleisiad.
    5. 14:00-14:25Drive to Brecon Canal Basin.
    6. 14:30-17:30Canal-basin walk, cafes and optional Dragonfly Cruise only when the current timetable fits.
    7. ReturnLeave Brecon around 17:30-18:00; arrive home approximately 18:45-19:30.
    8. TotalAbout 100-110 miles and 3-3.5 hours driving, plus walks and stops.

    If cloud, wind or rain makes the summit unsuitable, replace Pen y Fan with a lower viewpoint and spend longer in Brecon.

    Open day route Cruise timetable
    DAY 2

    Western Bannau Brycheiniog

    Llyn y Fan Fach · Carreg Cennen Castle

    1. 08:00Leave CF10 3EA for Llyn y Fan Fach. Allow 1 hr 40-2 hr; the final approach is narrow and slow.
    2. 10:00-13:00Short lake walk only: walk to the lake, enjoy the escarpment view and return the same way.
    3. LunchUse a packed lunch or stop in Llandovery or Llandeilo. Do not rely on facilities at the lake car park.
    4. 14:15-16:15Visit Carreg Cennen Castle, allowing time for the uphill approach and viewpoints.
    5. ReturnLeave by 16:30-17:00; arrive home approximately 18:15-19:00.
    6. TotalAbout 135-150 miles and 4-4.75 hours driving, plus walking.

    Do not add the full Llyn y Fan Fach ridge circuit on the same day as the castle. Roads are narrow and parking is limited.

    Open day route
    DAY 3

    Gower Peninsula

    Broughton Bay · Blue Pool · Rhossili Bay · Worm's Head

    1. 08:00-09:00Leave CF10 3EA according to the tide window. Allow 1 hr 40-2 hr 15 to Gower.
    2. Low tide AMVisit Broughton Bay and Blue Pool first, then lunch, Rhossili Bay and the Worm's Head clifftop viewpoint.
    3. Low tide PMStart at Rhossili viewpoint and beach, then visit Broughton and Blue Pool only with a safe return window.
    4. Worm's HeadThe viewpoint is straightforward. Crossing the causeway is a separate tidal walk.
    5. ReturnLeave Gower around 17:30-19:00; arrive home approximately 19:30-21:15.
    6. TotalAbout 145-160 miles and 4-5 hours driving, plus beach and cliff walks.

    Tides decide this day. Blue Pool and Worm's Head have cut-off risk; check tide times, sea state and local advice before going below cliffs.

    Low tide morning route Low tide afternoon route
    DAY 4

    Elan Valley

    Visitor Centre · Caban Coch · Garreg Ddu · Pen y Garreg · Craig Goch

    1. 07:45Leave CF10 3EA for Elan Valley Visitor Centre. Allow approximately 1 hr 55-2 hr 25.
    2. 10:15-11:00Visitor Centre, toilets, cafe, maps and first views of Caban Coch.
    3. 11:00-15:30Drive the dam sequence with short signed viewpoints and photo stops.
    4. OptionalAdd Claerwen Dam only in good weather and with enough daylight for the remote road.
    5. ReturnLeave Elan around 16:30-17:30; arrive home approximately 19:00-20:15.
    6. TotalAbout 160-180 miles and 4.5-5.5 hours driving; more with Claerwen.

    Expect remote roads, patchy mobile signal and exposed weather. Arrange fuel or charging before entering the valley. Keep Caerphilly Castle as a separate visit.

    Three-day alternatives

    Option A · Natural scenery priority

    1. Central Bannau: Pen y Fan, Craig Cerrig-gleisiad and Brecon Canal Basin.
    2. Carreg Cennen Castle and the short Llyn y Fan Fach lake walk.
    3. Gower Peninsula using the tide-led plan.

    Option B · Engineering and landscape priority

    1. Elan Valley dams as a full day.
    2. Central Bannau and Brecon Canal; Pen y Fan only if weather fits.
    3. Gower Peninsula using the tide-led plan.
